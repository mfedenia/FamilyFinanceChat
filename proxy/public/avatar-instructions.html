FamilyFinanceChat

Before your practice session

You’ll play the financial advisor in a voice conversation with a simulated family. Your goal is to learn what matters to the family. Use the fictional case details and leave out personal financial information, including your own income or account numbers.

How to open the conversation

Introduce yourself, then ask an open-ended question. Try one of these or use your own words.

“Thanks for meeting with me today. What would you most like to talk about?”

“What financial goal would you like your family to work toward first?”

Listen for a detail you can follow up on. If the family mentions saving for college, for example, you might ask, “What concerns you most about paying for college?” You can select End call whenever you’re ready to finish.
