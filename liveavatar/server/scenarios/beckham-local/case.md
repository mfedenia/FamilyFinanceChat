## Job descriptions

**Antonella — Senior Logistics Analyst, Rockwell Automation.** An individual-contributor project lead who
drives supply chain process improvement and cost control: leads cross-functional teams to identify, design
and implement improvements in distribution, transportation, inventory management and customer service;
knows the supply chain information systems and recommends new tools; monitors logistics effectiveness and
improves monthly reporting; coordinates IT, third parties, consultants, manufacturing, accounting and order
services; mentors team members; applies company policy (Standards of Business Conduct, ISO 9000/14000, etc.).
Qualifications include leading projects, Excel/Access, SQL, Power BI, SAP, Lean/Six Sigma; a bachelor's in
supply chain or engineering, master's (MBA or similar) preferred; limited travel.

**Tony — Academic Technology Specialist, UW-Milwaukee.** Supports faculty and students on learning management
systems (Canvas, Desire2Learn, Moodle); designs and builds multimedia course content (graphics, animation,
video, audio, simulations, web pages); lays out online courses; maintains instructional equipment and
software; produces audio-visual materials and supports classrooms, video conferencing and outreach events;
coaches and trains faculty one-on-one and in groups and provides live support. Expertise in instructional
design, HTML5/CSS, Flash, Section 508 accessibility, and good interpersonal and organizational skills.
