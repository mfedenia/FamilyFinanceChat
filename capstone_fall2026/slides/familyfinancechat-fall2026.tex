%== 5 == Workstream A =========================================================
\begin{frame}{First job: make it solid}
  \vspace{1.6em}
  \begin{center}
  \begin{minipage}{0.86\textwidth}\large
    \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.95em}
      \item Upgrade to the \hl{current version} --- carefully, one step at a time
      \item Give the professor a \hl{web address} instead of a laptop
      \item \hl{Automate} deployment and testing, so upgrades stop breaking things quietly
      \item \hl{Delete} the last remnants of the old fork
    \end{itemize}
  \end{minipage}
  \end{center}
  \vfill
  \note{Four items. Unglamorous, and it is what the course actually depends on. Do not go deeper -- the plan has the detail.}
\end{frame}

%== 6 == Workstream B =========================================================
\begin{frame}{Second job: give the client a face}
  \vfill
  \begin{center}
    {\Large Today the student \emph{types} to the client.}\\[1.3em]
    {\Huge\bfseries\color{ffcTeal} We want them to talk to it.}\\[1.3em]
    {\Large A face that listens, and answers out loud.}
  \end{center}
  \vfill
  \begin{center}\large
  You cannot rehearse a hard question about money\\[0.2em] when you have a backspace key.
  \end{center}
  \vfill
  \note{The pitch. Real advising happens out loud, in real time, with someone looking at you. Typing removes the exact pressure we are trying to teach.}
